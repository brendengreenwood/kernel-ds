## 2026-07-04 — Select ergonomics: full-width triggers, content-safe menus, padded lists

**What:** Three owner-reported issues with select menus, fixed in
`ui/select.tsx` so every consumer inherits them:
1. **Trigger width** — stock `w-fit` made selects hug their value ("corn ˅")
   while sibling inputs filled the form column. Now `w-full`, matching
   `Input`; compact call sites keep their explicit widths (tailwind-merge
   lets `w-40`/`w-28`/`w-auto` win).
2. **Menu width** — the popup was pinned to the trigger width
   (`w-(--anchor-width)` + `overflow-x-hidden`), so options wider than the
   trigger clipped. Now `min-w-[max(var(--anchor-width),9rem)]`: never
   narrower than the trigger or 144px, free to grow to its content.
3. **Internal padding** — items sat flush against the popup edge (the
   focus highlight bled into the rounded corner). The item list now
   carries `p-1` (moved off `SelectGroup` so bare items get it too) and
   items are `py-1.5 pl-2`.
**Verified:** headless — record-form Grain trigger 264px = its column,
menu ≥ trigger with 4px insets and no clipped item text; 112px operator

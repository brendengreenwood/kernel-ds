Floating content goes in boxes, and headers get a third size

Two headers floating on the well over two panels is four objects whose
relationships are carried entirely by vertical gaps. It reads while it is small
and stops reading the moment anything else is added: nothing on the well says
which header owns which panel except how close they are, and the well is where
every future block will also want to float. Boxes state the grouping
structurally, so the layout survives the next block instead of being retuned by
it.

This reverses the "outside" half of register 5.14, recorded as a supersede
rather than an edit. That entry's argument -- a title inside the box it titles
reads as the box's first row -- is right, but it is answered by giving the title
its own size rather than its own position.

So PageHeader takes a size: page 44/30, panel 36/20, section 28/16, chip and
title falling together so the mark keeps its relationship to the words. The
condensed boolean could not carry a third step and is gone. Heading level does
not follow size -- page is the h1 and both smaller sizes are h2, because the
panels of an opened row are peer sections of that row, and choosing a level for
its type size is how a document grows a heading hierarchy that does not match
its structure.

Three things fell out of it. Only the first panel breathes: the edge pulse is an
arrival signal, and two panels running it states one message twice. The tiles'
corner became panel-radius minus panel-inset, the same arithmetic as the
activity table's inner surface, because it is now the same relationship. And the
tiles' 12-unit text inset went to 5 -- that inset existed to align a card's
label with a header floating above it, which was alignment doing a box's job,
and inside a panel it left a 94px text column that wrapped "Bushels bought" and
knocked its figure off the row's line.

Producers' OfferInset gets the same treatment. The register calls these two the
same object built twice, so a layout only one of them has is a difference nobody
chose. Its figures moved into the header's action slot and dropped from 24px to
18px: a 24px figure beside a 16px section title inverts them and the aside
becomes the loudest thing in the panel.

Measured: panel over well 1.28:1 dark and 1.26:1 light, tile hairline on panel
1.44 and 1.36, section title 14.34 and 17.5, description and caption 5.79 and
7.11, chips 42/35/27px at 14/12.08/10.16px radius, titles 30/20/16, all four
tile figures back on one line.

check-drift-register refused both new rows for being inserted above their
predecessors, which is the third time it has caught a numbering fault before a
merge did.

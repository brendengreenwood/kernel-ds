Opening a row moves the view to it

Trimming the roll-up bought 68px and the activity table still opened below the
fold. Chasing it with layout would have kept losing: the panel is sized by what
it has to say, and the viewport is not a constraint it can be argued down to.
The view was pointed at the wrong part of the page, so the view moves.

Three rules keep the scroll from being worse than the problem. It moves the
smaller of two distances -- enough to show the panel's bottom, or enough to put
the row at the top -- so a panel taller than the screen scrolls to the row and
stops rather than running the row off the top edge to chase a bottom that will
never fit. It does not move at all when the panel is already visible, because a
view that jumps on every click teaches the reader to brace for it. And
collapsing never scrolls: the row the reader is looking at is the row they just
closed, and moving the page out from under a close is how a list loses someone's
place.

useLayoutEffect rather than useEffect, so the measurement lands after the panel
is laid out and before the browser paints. The effect body is a block, never a
concise arrow: window.scrollBy returns a promise in current Chrome, and a
concise arrow hands React that promise where it expects a cleanup function --
the exact fault that blanked the whole app once. Second sighting, so the warning
now lives in the hook's own comment.

Both row tables get it. They are the same object built twice, and a behaviour
that exists on one of them is a bug on the other.

Measured: /scenarios row 7 from 616px off-screen to 51px with its panel fully in
view; /producers row 9 from 833px to 178px, panel bottom 711 of 720; a forced
380px viewport against a 583px panel lands the row at 16px and holds it there;
already-visible rows and collapses verified still.
